# Citylink Group Deals / Collective Demand

**Status: PRIMARY FEATURE**

## 1. Core Idea

Citylink aggregates demand instead of directly providing every service.

``` text
Individual demand
Individual demand
Individual demand
        ↓
     Citylink
        ↓
Aggregated demand
        ↓
      Vendor
        ↓
Potential efficiency / group pricing
```

## 2. Examples

### Salon-at-home

15 women in a society request same-day service.

### Sofa/carpet cleaning

Multiple homes in one building request cleaning.

### Appliance AMC

20--30 flats collectively request AC/RO/geyser maintenance.

## 3. Product Principle

Citylink is the demand aggregation layer.

## 4. Creation

Creator specifies: - Need/service - City - Community optional - Area -
Target participants - Deadline - Preferred date - Preferred slot -
Notes - Optional target budget

## 5. Participation

Users can join and provide: - Quantity - Preferred slot - Address when
required - Notes

Exact address must remain protected.

## 6. States

-   Draft
-   Open
-   Target reached
-   Offers available
-   Offer selected
-   Booking
-   In progress
-   Completed
-   Cancelled
-   Disputed

## 7. Pricing States

### User target

Desired price.

### Potential group price

An estimate/offer that is not final.

### Confirmed price

Accepted final price.

Never visually confuse them.

## 8. Vendor Discovery

Match vendors by: - City - Service - Service area - Availability -
Business status

## 9. Offer

Vendor submits: - Price per unit - Total price - Availability -
Validity - Terms - Message

## 10. Selection

The documented selection mechanism determines which offer becomes
accepted.

## 11. Booking

Each participant can have: - Deal - Vendor offer - Final price - Slot -
Booking status

## 12. Scheduling

Support: - Shared slot - Multiple slots - Participant-specific slots

## 13. Cancellation

Explicit policies must cover: - User cancellation - Vendor
cancellation - Deal not reaching threshold - Platform cancellation -
Safety cancellation

## 14. Vendor Protection

Protect against: - Fake demand - Spam - No-shows - Non-payment - Abuse

## 15. Customer Protection

Protect against: - Fake vendors - Unexpected pricing - No-shows - Poor
fulfillment - Hidden charges

## 16. Disputes

Track: - Deal - Booking - Offer - Evidence - Status - Resolution

## 17. Reviews

After completion, participants can review the vendor/service.

## 18. Notifications

Examples: - Someone joined. - Target is near. - Vendor submitted an
offer. - Offer selected. - Booking confirmed. - Reminder. - Completed.

## 19. Metrics

Track: - Deals created - Participants - Target completion - Vendor
response - Offers - Completion - Cancellation - Verified savings

Do not claim savings without a trustworthy basis.

## 20. Visibility

Group Deals should be prominent in: - Home - Discover - Communities -
Business dashboard - City pages
