# Citylink Development Roadmap

## Strategy

``` text
Foundation
→ Social
→ Communities
→ Group Deals
→ Business
→ Marketplace
→ Services
→ Jobs
→ Events
→ Intelligence
→ Monetization
→ Scale
```

## MVP

Essential: - Authentication - Profiles - City switching - Feed - Posts -
Search - Communities - Messaging - Business profiles - Group Deals -
Vendor dashboard - Notifications - Admin - Reporting/moderation

## Stage 1: Foundation

Authentication, users, profiles, cities, database, APIs, security,
storage, notifications and analytics foundation.

## Stage 2: Social

Feed, posts, comments, reactions, follow, profiles and search.

## Stage 3: Communities

Public/private communities, membership, moderation, announcements, polls
and events.

## Stage 4: Group Deals

Demand, participants, vendors, offers, pricing, selection, booking,
completion, reviews and disputes.

## Stage 5: Business

Business profile, dashboard, team, services, products, leads, Group Deal
opportunities, reviews and analytics.

## Stage 6: Marketplace

Listings, search, filters, seller profiles, messaging and reports.

## Stage 7: Services

Providers, services, service areas, pricing, booking and reviews.

## Stage 8: Jobs

Jobs, search, applications and employer management.

## Stage 9: Events

Events, discovery, RSVP, notifications and management.

## Stage 10: Intelligence

AI search, recommendations, Group Deal matching, business matching and
moderation assistance.

## Stage 11: Monetization

Potential: - Business subscriptions - Lead fees - Group Deal fees -
Promoted placement - Event fees - Marketplace fees

## Stage 12: Scale

Only after real traffic: - Cache - Search infrastructure - Queues -
CDN - Read replicas - Dedicated services

## Launch Strategy

Start with one city.

Recommended pilot: **Muzaffarpur**

Seed: - Communities - Colleges - Societies - Businesses - Service
providers - Group Deals

## Growth Loop

``` text
User
→ Community
→ Local opportunity
→ Group Deal
→ Invite
→ Vendor
→ More value
→ More users
```

## Metrics

-   Activation
-   Day 1 retention
-   Day 7 retention
-   Day 30 retention
-   Active communities
-   Group Deals created
-   Group Deals completed
-   Vendor response
-   Business activity
-   Successful local connections

## Release Strategy

Alpha → Closed beta → City beta → Public launch.

## Avoid Premature Complexity

Do not prioritize: - Full delivery infrastructure - Large ad platform -
Livestreaming - Excessive AI - Complex financial infrastructure -
Premature microservices
