# Citylink Phases

## Phase Management

Each phase records: - Objective - Requirements - Decisions -
Dependencies - Open questions - Completed items - Deferred items - Final
status

## Phase 1: Product Foundation

Established the initial Citylink concept, vision, problem and city-based
platform direction.

## Phase 2: Users, Personas & Journeys

Defined major users and basic journeys.

Main users: - Residents - Students - Businesses - Communities -
Opportunity seekers

Phase 2 was accepted as a strong foundation.

## Phase 3: Core Product Experience

Expanded the product experience and major feature concepts, creating the
connected local/social direction.

## Phase 4: Information Architecture

Focused on how the expanding feature set should be organized into an
understandable product.

## Phase 5: Consumer App

The main app structure was defined around: - Onboarding/auth - Home -
Discover/search - Marketplace - Jobs - Property-related opportunities -
Services - Business discovery - Communities/social content - Events -
Chat - Notifications - AI opportunities - Profile

## Phase 6: Business Dashboard

Business functionality was established as a dedicated platform
experience with: - Business profiles - Dashboard - Analytics - CRM/lead
management - Advertising/monetization concepts

The main consumer app planning was considered complete through Phase 6
before the Admin Panel was treated separately.

## Phase 7: Admin Panel

Admin Panel became a separate platform.

Focus: - Users - Businesses - Moderation - Reports - Verification -
Analytics - Operations

## Phase 9: Platform Expansion

Expanded the product toward a broader city ecosystem, local discovery
and connected opportunities.

## Phase 10: Three-Platform Model

Citylink was explicitly defined as:

1.  Consumer app
2.  Business profile web / business dashboard
3.  Admin panel

All share the same backend/data ecosystem.

A business presence is connected to the user account model rather than
requiring an unrelated identity.

## Phase 11: Business Ecosystem

Expanded: - Business profile - Business dashboard - Leads - Services -
Products - Reviews - Analytics - Business opportunities

## Phase 12: Platform Integration

Connected consumer, business and admin ecosystems through shared backend
data and workflows.

## Phase 13: Community System

Community became a primary platform concept.

Examples: - Muzaffarpur Community: public - MIT College: private and
limited to verified students

## Phase 14: Group Deals

Group Buy / collective demand became a primary feature.

Examples: - Salon-at-home group booking - Sofa/carpet cleaning -
Appliance AMC - AC/RO/geyser maintenance

The platform aggregates demand and connects it to vendors.

## Phase 15: Backend & Data Architecture

Defined major data domains: - Users - Profiles - Cities - Communities -
Posts - Businesses - Marketplace - Services - Jobs - Events - Group
Deals - Vendor offers - Bookings - Messaging - Notifications - Reviews -
Reports - Verification - Analytics - Search - Payments

## Phase 16: Security, Privacy, Trust & Safety

Defined: - Authentication - Authorization - Privacy - Location
protection - Business verification - Community verification -
Reporting - Moderation - Fraud detection - Anti-spam - Review
integrity - Group Deal protection - Vendor/customer protection - Payment
security - Admin security - Audit logs - Account deletion

## Phase 17: Development Roadmap & Launch

Strategy: - Foundation - Social core - Communities - Group Deals -
Business ecosystem - Marketplace - Services - Jobs - Events -
Intelligence - Monetization - Scale

Launch one city first, validate the model, then expand.

## Phase 18: UI/UX Design System

Focus: - Product personality - Colors - Typography - Components -
Navigation - Motion - Accessibility - Mobile - Public web - Business
dashboard - Admin panel - Design tokens
