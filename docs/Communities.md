# Citylink Communities

## 1. Purpose

Communities are first-class spaces for people connected by city,
institution, society, neighborhood or interest.

## 2. Examples

### Muzaffarpur Community

Public city community.

### MIT College

Private community restricted to verified MIT students.

## 3. Types

At minimum: - Public - Private

A hidden/restricted discovery mode may be used for sensitive
communities.

## 4. Public

Discoverable by users. Membership can still have rules.

## 5. Private

Content is protected. Membership requires approval/verification
according to configuration.

## 6. Creation

Creator configures: - Name - Description - City - Category - Privacy -
Rules - Verification - Cover - Avatar

## 7. Roles

-   Owner
-   Admin
-   Moderator
-   Member

## 8. Membership States

-   Requested
-   Approved
-   Rejected
-   Banned
-   Left

## 9. Content

-   Posts
-   Comments
-   Reactions
-   Announcements
-   Polls
-   Events
-   Group Deals

## 10. Moderation

Community staff can: - Remove content - Mute - Remove members - Ban -
Review reports

Citylink retains platform-level authority.

## 11. Verification

Possible: - Institutional email - Student ID - Administrator
verification

## 12. Privacy

Private content must not appear in public search or unauthorized APIs.

## 13. Discovery

Users can discover communities through: - City - Interest - Search -
Recommendations - Events - Group Deals
