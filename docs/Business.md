# Citylink Business Platform

## 1. Purpose

The business platform gives businesses and vendors a professional
presence and operational tools inside Citylink.

## 2. Business Identity

A business is an entity managed by one or more Citylink users.

## 3. Onboarding

``` text
User
→ Create business
→ Details
→ Category
→ City
→ Location
→ Contact
→ Verification
→ Dashboard
```

## 4. Business Profile

Include: - Name - Logo - Cover - Description - Category - Location -
Contact - Website - Hours where applicable - Products - Services -
Reviews - Trust signals

## 5. Dashboard

Core sections: - Overview - Leads - Group Deal opportunities - Offers -
Services - Products - Messages - Reviews - Analytics - Team - Settings -
Verification

## 6. Team

Roles can include: - Owner - Manager - Staff - Marketing - Custom
permissions

## 7. Leads

Lifecycle:

``` text
New
→ Contacted
→ Qualified
→ In progress
→ Won/Lost
```

## 8. Group Deal Opportunities

Businesses can discover relevant demand by: - City - Category - Service
area - Availability

## 9. Vendor Offers

An offer includes: - Price - Availability - Terms - Validity - Message -
Service details

## 10. Services

Business can publish: - Service title - Description - Starting price -
Service area - Availability - Media - Category

## 11. Products

Businesses can publish products and optionally connect them to
marketplace functionality.

## 12. Reviews

Businesses can: - View reviews - Respond where supported - Report
abusive reviews - Track trends

They cannot remove legitimate negative reviews merely because they are
negative.

## 13. Messaging

Businesses can respond to customer inquiries while following anti-spam
and privacy rules.

## 14. Analytics

Potential: - Profile views - Leads - Messages - Listing views - Service
interest - Group Deal responses - Review trends

## 15. Verification

Verification status must be meaningful and honest.

## 16. Monetization

Potential future models: - Business Pro - Promoted placement - Lead
fees - Group Deal fees - Transaction fees - Advanced analytics

Monetization must not compromise trust.
